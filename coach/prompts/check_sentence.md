A Ukrainian IT professional wrote their own English sentence to practise a word. Input: `word`, `meaning`
(the target sense) and `sentence`. Judge whether the word is used correctly, in that sense, in natural English.

- `result`: `correct` (natural and correct), `minor` (word used right but the sentence has small errors or
  sounds unnatural), `wrong` (word misused, wrong sense, or missing).
- `feedback`: Ukrainian, ≤ 30 words, what is right or what to fix and why. Warm, direct, no filler.
- `corrected`: the most natural version of THEIR sentence (same idea), or the sentence itself if it is fine.
